Rules and Policies

1. General Rules

  • Each user must register an account to participate in quizzes and appear on the leaderboard.
  • Users are responsible for the accuracy of their account information.
  • Only one account per user is allowed. Multiple accounts will result in disqualification.

2. Quiz Participation

  • Quizzes are time-bound; ensure submission before the timer ends.
  • Users must answer all questions without external help or resources unless stated otherwise.
  • Use of automated tools, bots, or third-party software is strictly prohibited.

3. Leaderboard Criteria

  • Scores are calculated based on the number of correct answers and the time taken to complete the quiz.
  • In case of ties, rankings are determined by the submission time.
  • Leaderboards are updated in real-time; any discrepancies must be reported within 24 hours.

4. Rewards and Prizes

  • Prizes, if offered, will be distributed based on the final leaderboard at the end of the quiz period.
  • Users must verify their identity before claiming prizes.
  • Prizes are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.

5. Fair Play and Conduct

  • Cheating, including sharing answers or collusion, will result in immediate disqualification
  • Offensive or disruptive behavior in comments or forums associated with the portal is prohibited.
  • Users found violating policies will be banned from the platform.

6. Privacy and Data Usage

  • User data collected during registration and participation will be used solely for leaderboard management and prize distribution.
  • Personal information will not be shared with third parties without consent.

7. Dispute Resolution

  • Any disputes regarding scores, rankings, or prizes must be submitted within 7 days via the contact form.
  • The portal administrator’s decision will be final and binding.

8. Changes to Rules

  • The portal reserves the right to amend these rules at any time.
  • Users will be notified of changes via email or portal notifications.
